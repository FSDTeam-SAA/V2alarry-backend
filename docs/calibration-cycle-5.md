The October 29 reliability correction adds one conditional, focused clarity check before otherwise eligible closing/takeaway turns. It introduces no graph stage, dependency, provider, public API/SSE change or database migration.

The private extraction contract supports independent `status_basis`/`status_evidence` and `readiness_basis`/`readiness_evidence`, normalized through the existing source, identity and transition checks. Legacy single-basis updates remain readable. The existing turn assessment also classifies an interpretation gap from raw client evidence; an extractor's classification does not prove the client recognizes the distinction. The server gives an unresolved observation/interpretation distinction priority without treating the client's own feelings as disputed facts.

Closing moves are nonexecuting, even if the model proposes an action stage. Assessed closure evidence identifies its own action, so an unready alternative cannot block a different chosen step. `invite_takeaways` is retained as the existing `close_or_continue` category in durable state for older readers; the protected trace retains the exact submove. Temporary closure proof never becomes a durable client trait. Exact-source validation establishes attribution, not semantic entailment; actual replies still require review.

The turn assessment selects raw evidence for the chosen step and willingness. Once existing policy, identity, readiness and discussion gates permit `close_or_continue` or `invite_takeaways`, a focused structured call reads only raw client messages and selected action identity. It independently distinguishes understanding from choice alone, selecting an exact client insight clause. This can reject a plan-only claim or recover an account already supplied earlier. The broader assessment's labels and reasoning are excluded from that check. Source binding and the selected action remain enforced; semantic classification remains probabilistic and requires actual-reply review. An unchosen possibility cannot close, and explicit stopping skips the check.

The focused check has one application-level attempt, bounded by the existing 30-second assessment timeout pattern. Failure withholds closure while preserving valid facts and action state. Its `closure_review` result and duration stay in the protected turn trace and are excluded from durable state, generation, summaries and subsequent assessment input. No raw error messages or new external telemetry are introduced. The extra call is justified by recorded failures of the original and finer single-assessment clarity classifications; cost/latency and positive/negative controls are recorded with the correction evidence.

A clarification of the help needed no longer automatically repeats an understanding check after an unhelpful confirmation. Genuine factual correction can still request a synthesis repair. Reflection uses only the client's own account and possible approaches; it cannot introduce a method or tip.

Summary output schemas restrict commitment operations to current permissions: preserve during exploration/preparation, preserve or clear after a current evidenced withdrawal, and replacement only when commitment recording is permitted. Exact current quotes, matching prior values and action references remain checked after parsing. Invalid summary revisions receive at most one recovery within the existing summary operation. Valid summaries still use one call. Extraction/assessment retries remain bounded. The conditional clarity check precedes generation; streaming itself remains direct. The isolated runtime verifier checks every turn's summary freshness, nonexecution and valid optional state rather than requiring a particular conversational move or mandatory confirmation; its contract checks are separate from coaching-quality review.

The existing turn assessment now selects bounded progress, explicit/natural supported understanding, and separate support permission. Understanding is established from coaching-user evidence, retained through compatible details, and marked for revision after material correction. Legacy tested-understanding flags remain readable but cannot stand in for a missing supported picture in the new positive-transition path.

Coach-written examples, suggestions and framework teaching are deferred in this calibration, including when requested. `teach_selectively` remains readable in older state but is normalized to client-led discovery/exploration.

`support_choice` permits client-owned exploration or requested preparation in possibilities. Preparation uses the client's own language while preserving unreadiness for the real action. It cannot authorize execution. `invite_takeaways` invites the client's missing account of what became clearer and what they will do with it after a step is chosen. An account already supplied spontaneously must not be requested again. `close_or_continue` requires validated raw client evidence of clarity, the chosen step and willingness to try, attached to the matching planned/ongoing action. The client may always stop without fulfilling those conditions. A learning or research step can be sufficient; the future outcome need not be known.

Action IDs, lifecycle status, and exact evidence remain durable. Action readiness and preparation/discussion refusals have independent proof. New scoped permission updates preserve readiness for the real action. Legacy unknown scope can be classified only using that action's original boundary evidence. Preparation and discussion refusals, abandonment, and explicit stopping remain binding. A considered action cannot become a confirmed commitment through wording requests or generic ownership flags.

Combined Cycle 4/5 review additionally enforces these distinctions: exploration cannot authorize practice or execution; preparation/topic permission updates cannot change action status; independent scopes merge onto the same action ID; topic refusals also block conceptual teaching on that topic; denied execution permission cannot consolidate commitment. A new-path summary replacement must reference the matching planned/ongoing action. The legacy explicit-confirmation-only prompt rule is superseded by natural or explicit evidence-supported understanding.

Only compact session understanding and scoped boundary context are added to durable JSON. `progress_assessment`, `support_assessment` and `closure_assessment` are transient and excluded from durable state and CCE prompts. Closure evidence remains in the protected turn trace. A client-evidenced commitment can be retained at valid closure without authorizing further execution. Protected decision traces identify `calibration-5`; public response shapes are unchanged. JSON and streaming generation share validated context and policy; policy downgrades must be idempotent.
